## Pages are the unit of work

A storage engine reads and writes fixed-size pages. A B-tree node therefore needs a compact header, a slot directory, keys, values, and enough free space for future inserts.

## Make writes recoverable

Write-ahead logging records an intended page change before the changed page reaches durable storage. During recovery, the log supplies the history needed to redo safe changes.

## Splits are a coordination problem

When a leaf fills, split it and publish the new separator to its parent. Latches protect each local edit, while the logging protocol protects the change across crashes.
