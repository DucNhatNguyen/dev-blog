## A ring is only the starting point

Consistent hashing minimizes movement when nodes join or leave, but a single position per node often creates uneven load. Virtual nodes smooth that distribution.

## Bound the hot spots

For workloads with skewed keys, use a bounded-load strategy that skips nodes already above a target load. The ring chooses candidates; load information makes the final choice practical.

## Make ownership inspectable

Expose ring membership, replica placement, and movement estimates. Operators should be able to explain why a key moved before a rebalance becomes an incident.
